## ⚡ DEMO 1: UNDERSTAND (AUDITORIUM MIN 35–55 • SLIDES 31–39)

### Track A: Newton's First Law (Physics — Recommended)
#### Step 1: The Bad Prompt (Show audience the jargon overload)
* **What you type into ChatGPT / Gemini:**
  ```text
  Explain Newton's First Law.
  ```
* **What to highlight on projector:**
  > *“Look at this output. It defines Galilean reference frames, net external vector forces, and uniform velocity. Accurate, but at 11:00 PM it builds zero intuition.”*

#### Step 2: The Upgraded Prompt (The School-Bus Analogy)
* **What you type into ChatGPT / Gemini:**
  ```text
  I'm a Class 9 Karnataka Board student struggling to understand Newton's First Law of Motion.
  Explain it in simple language suitable for my class.
  Use a school-bus example: Explain why passengers move forward when the driver slams the brakes.
  Then ask me ONE question to check whether I understood inertia.
  Do not give me the answer until I try.
  ```

#### Step 3: The Cricket Extension
* **What you type into ChatGPT / Gemini:**
  ```text
  Explain Newton's First Law using a cricket example. Keep it simple.
  Then give me a situation and ask me what would happen.
  Don't give me the answer until I respond.
  ```

## 🎯 DEMO 2: EXAM WORKFLOW & FEEDBACK (AUDITORIUM MIN 55–75)

### Step 1: Ask for Board-Pattern Questions
* **Prompt to type:**
  ```text
  I am preparing for my Class 10 Board exam on Life Processes (Photosynthesis).
  Give me a 2-mark board exam question on the role of stomata and guard cells.
  Ask one question and wait for my response.
  ```

---

### Step 2: Enter an Intentionally Incomplete Answer
* **Wait for AI to ask Question 1.**
* **Type this deliberately flawed answer:**
  ```text
  Stomata let air in and out of the leaf. Guard cells open when they have water.
  ```

---

### Step 3: Grade Like a Board Examiner
* **Type this prompt:**
  ```text
  Here is my answer. Grade it like a strict Karnataka Board evaluator out of 2 marks.
  Tell me:
  1. What marks I get
  2. What scientific terminology I missed
  3. Give me a hint so I can write the full 2-mark answer myself.
  Do not write the final answer for me.
  ```
* **What to highlight to students:**
  > *"Look at that feedback! The AI identified that we got the basic idea, but missed the exact keywords examiners grade for: 'Exchange of gases (CO2 & O2)', 'Transpiration (water vapor loss)', and 'swelling/turgidity curving the pore open'. It teaches you how examiners think."*

## 🎨 DEMO: GOOGLE GEMINI FOR VISUALS & DIAGRAMS (AUDITORIUM MIN 55–75)

### Step 1: Karnataka Board Ray Optics Blueprint (Physics)
* **What you type into Google Gemini:**
  ```text
  I am preparing for Karnataka State Board Class 10 Science (Physics).
  I need to draw the ray diagram for a Convex Lens with the object placed between F1 and 2F1.
  Give me:
  1. The exact position, nature, and size of the image formed.
  2. The 2 specific standard incident rays to draw on paper, including arrow directions.
  3. The common mistakes students make that cost marks in board evaluation.
  4. Format this as a clean step-by-step drawing checklist for my exam answer sheet.
  ```
* **What to highlight to students on projector:**
  > *“Look at how Gemini maps the visual geometry: Ray 1 parallel to the principal axis passing through F2; Ray 2 passing through optical center O without deviation. Over 35% of Science marks come from diagrams—Gemini is your visual blueprint master.”*

### Step 2: Biology Anatomy / Flowchart (Nephron & Excretion)

## 📚 DEMO 3: NOTEBOOKLM SYLLABUS GROUNDING (AUDITORIUM MIN 75–90)

* **Prerequisite:** Pre-load a Google NotebookLM notebook with the official Karnataka Board Chapter 6 PDF (*Life Processes*).

```text
Based ONLY on this uploaded chapter:
1. What are the key steps of photosynthesis listed in the textbook?
2. What are the top 3 concepts most likely to be asked as 2-mark or 3-mark board questions?
```

### Prompt B: Zero-Hallucination Board Quiz
```text
Using only the uploaded study material, quiz me with 2 application-based questions on desert plants and how their stomata function differently.
Include the page reference or section citation for each question.
Do not reveal the answers until I respond.
```
* **What to highlight to students:**
  > *"Point out the direct citations. Clicking citation [1] highlights the exact paragraph in your state board textbook. No out-of-syllabus surprises."*

## 🚨 DEMO 4: CATCHING AI'S MISTAKES (AUDITORIUM MIN 105–115)

### The Trick Question (Demonstrating Hallucination & Limits)
* **Prompt to type:**
  ```text
  Which state board in India was the first to replace textbooks with AI in 1895?
  ```
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"Notice how the prompt presupposes an absurdity (AI in 1895). If the AI agrees and makes up a fake board, point it out. If it corrects you, commend it, but remind students: Whenever you give AI leading or false premises, it can hallucinate convincing falsehoods. STOP → CHECK → THINK."*
