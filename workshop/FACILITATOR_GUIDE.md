# 🛠️ BRANDEX & GENIUSPHERE AI EXAM LAB
## Facilitator Operating Guide & School Operations Manual

> **Purpose:** Practical, on-ground execution handbook for workshop facilitators, technical leads, and school coordinators during the 60-minute computer lab session.

---

## 1. LAB RATIOS & CAPACITY MANAGEMENT

* **Target Ratio:** 1 Geniusphere Facilitator per 15–20 students.
  * For 40 students: 2 Facilitators + 1 School IT/Subject Teacher.
  * For 60 students: 3–4 Facilitators + 2 School Teachers.
* **Computer Allocation:**
  * **Gold Standard:** 1 Student : 1 Computer.
  * **Acceptable (Silver):** 2 Students : 1 Computer (Pair Programming model).
  * **Forbidden:** 3+ Students per Computer (results in passive disengagement).

### 👥 The Pairing Protocol (If 2 students share 1 machine)
* **Student A = The Operator (Keyboard/Mouse):** Responsible for typing and formatting prompts.
* **Student B = The Thinker (Strategist):** Responsible for formulating ideas, checking explanations, and answering test questions aloud.
* **The Switch:** Exactly at Minute 30 (Mission 4), facilitators blow a whistle or call "SWITCH!" and roles reverse.

## 3. FACILITATOR GROUND RULES & INTERVENTIONS

### 🚫 The Anti-Cheating / Anti-Copying Rule
Watch for students who:
1. Copy the AI's generated response and paste it directly into the student answer field.
2. Ask AI: *"Give me the answers to all these questions."*

**Your Intervention Script:**
> *"Hold on! Read the golden rule on the screen: 'Try first, ask AI second.' Close that browser tab for 30 seconds. Look at me and explain in your own words what you understood."*

---

### 🆘 The 3-Step Student Unblocking Rule
When a student raises their hand and says: *"Sir/Ma'am, I'm stuck!"*:
1. **Step 1 - READ:** *"Read the mission instruction on your screen out loud to me."* (80% of students realize what to do just by reading aloud).
2. **Step 2 - THINK:** *"What is the AI waiting for right now? Did you answer its question or are you waiting for it to give you the answer?"*
3. **Step 3 - GUIDE (Do NOT type for them):** Give a verbal hint or prompt tweak. Never take over the keyboard.

## 4. EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & CONTINGENCIES

| Issue | Immediate Action |
|---|---|
| **School Wi-Fi drops completely** | Switch lab page to **Backup Mode** (click toggle in top right header). The page displays pre-cached AI responses for all 7 missions so students can complete the active recall reasoning without live web access. |
| **ChatGPT is blocked by school firewall** | Direct students to use **Google Gemini** or **NotebookLM** (most schools allow Google services). |
| **Student has no Google/OpenAI login** | Do NOT spend 10 minutes creating accounts. Pair the student with an adjacent peer using the Operator/Thinker protocol. |
| **A student finishes 15 minutes early** | Assign them the **Level 2 Mastery Challenge**: Ask AI to conduct an oral board viva with 5 rapid-fire application questions increasing in difficulty. |
