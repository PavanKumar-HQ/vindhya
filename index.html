🏆 THE 5 GOLDEN RULES OF STUDYING WITH AI

  • 01
    Never Ask for the Answer: Passive reading creates an illusion of knowing. Always ask AI to quiz you and grade your mistakes.
  • 02
    Ground Your Source: Use NotebookLM with your exact Karnataka State Board / NCERT textbook PDF to prevent hallucinations.
  • 03
    The 5-Part Formula: Always specify: [WHO I AM] + [WHAT TOPIC] + [BOARD STYLE] + [HOW] + [RULE].
  • 04
    Scientific Keywords Rule: Board evaluators grade strictly on keywords (e.g. “inertia of motion”, not “it pushes them”).
  • 05
    The 15-Minute Daily Drill: 5m concept breakdown → 5m active retrieval quiz → 5m self-correction.

🎯 1. THE STRICT BOARD EXAMINER PROMPT

Forces AI to grade your handwritten or typed attempt against official marking keys:

I am a Karnataka State Board Class [9/10] student preparing for Science.
Grade this answer out of [2/3] marks strictly like a board evaluator:
1. Marks awarded out of total
2. Missing scientific keywords or technical terms
3. One precise hint for me to rewrite it myself.
My Answer: [Paste answer]

🧠 2. THE SOCRATIC TUTOR (NO ANSWERS GIVEN)

Makes AI act like a private tutor who asks guided questions one step at a time:

You are my Karnataka Board Science tutor. I am struggling with [Topic].
RULE: Do NOT explain everything at once. Do NOT give me the final answer.
Ask me ONE diagnostic question to test what I already know.
Wait for my reply before asking the next step.

💡 3. THE ANALOGY & MENTAL MODEL BUILDER

Demystifies abstract concepts with memorable real-world mechanics:

Explain [e.g. Electric Potential Difference / Refraction] to a 15-year-old student using an everyday physical analogy (like water flowing in pipes or a car moving from tarmac into mud).
After the analogy, connect every part directly to the official textbook definition and SI unit.

📚 4. NOTEBOOKLM SOURCE-GROUNDED DRILL

Guarantees zero hallucinations by binding exclusively to your uploaded notes:

Based STRICTLY on the uploaded Karnataka Board textbook chapter:
1. List the top 5 high-frequency questions likely to appear for 3 or 4 marks.
2. Generate a 5-question fill-in-the-blank quiz on key definitions and SI units.
3. Do not include answers—quiz me first.

⚡ 5. KEYWORD DIAGNOSTIC AUDITOR

Audits your written answer against textbook key terms before you walk into exam hall:

Here is a board question: [Question]
Here is my drafted response: [Answer]
Highlight every essential keyword present in green.
List in red every critical scientific phrase that was omitted and must be included for full marks.
