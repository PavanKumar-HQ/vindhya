# GENIUSPHERE AI EXAM LAB
## Complete 71-Slide Presentation Production Plan & Master Playbook

> **Target Audience:** Karnataka State Board & CBSE Classes 9 & 10  
> **Session Format:** 120-Minute Auditorium Masterclass + 60-Minute Digital Computer Lab  
> **Core Loop:** Hook → Students Respond → You Reveal → Demo → Students React → Challenge → Next Hook  
> **Golden Principle:** Hard rule of an active interaction every 7–10 minutes. No continuous lectures.  
> **Slide Types:**
> - **Type A:** Big Statement (Impactful typography, high contrast)
> - **Type B:** Interaction (A/B/C/D votes, hands up, room shout-outs)
> - **Type C:** Demo & Comparison (Prompt vs Response vs Learning)
> - **Type D:** Process & Flow (Horizontal or vertical sequence maps)
> - **Type E:** Challenge & Inspection (Spot errors, fix bad prompts, human vs AI)

  ```text

  Learn with AI. Don't let AI learn for you.
  Geniusphere
  ```
* **Visual:** Minimal Swiss editorial layout. Central loop diagram: `STUDENT → AI → THINK → ANSWER → LEARN`. No cartoon robots.
* **Presenter Script (What you say):**
  > “Good morning everyone! Walk to center stage. Look out across the room.  
  > Before we start today, I want to make one thing completely clear: I am not going to lecture you on what AI stands for. I’m not going to bore you with definitions from 1950.

* **Teaching Takeaway:** Sets the adult, respectful, serious tone of the masterclass.
